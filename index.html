Built for match day

Everything a pickleball host needs.

Every court included

Run one court or ten, with automatic rest rotation — multi-court is never a paid add-on here.

Handles dropouts

Someone rolls an ankle or heads home early? Sit them out from the next round, or re-draw the current one without them — every remaining round still balances.

Knows when you're done

Smash! works out the ideal number of rounds so everyone plays evenly, and flags the natural finish.

Smart matchmaking

Every round is planned ahead so partners and opponents spread evenly — with 8 players, everyone partners with everyone exactly once.

Live leaderboard

Ranked by points, then point difference, then wins — updating after every match.

Match timer

A round countdown that beeps and vibrates when time's up — and the screen stays awake while you score.

Works offline

No account, no internet. Auto-saves every 2 seconds and nothing leaves your phone.

Archives, rematch & backup

Every tournament saved with full scoreboards — flip Fair Ending on to compare projected and actual scores. Replay a line-up in one tap, or back it all up to a file.

Export & share

Save the schedule and results as PDF or CSV, or share the standings anywhere.
