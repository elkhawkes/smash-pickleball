Everything a pickleball host needs.

Every court included

Run one court or ten, with automatic rest rotation — multi-court is never a paid add-on here.

Late arrivals & dropouts

Someone turns up late? Add them mid-tournament and they play every round until they've caught up. Remove a no-show in one tap, or sit out an injured player — Smash! tells you when games will even out.

Swap or re-draw a round

Need to split a pair or bring someone in? Tap a name to swap players before the round is played, or re-draw the whole round.

Smart matchmaking

Every round is planned ahead so partners and opponents spread evenly — with 8 players, everyone partners with everyone exactly once. Whoever has played least always plays next.

Knows when games even out

Smash! works out when everyone will have played the same number of games and flags the natural finish. If they can't even out, Fair Ending keeps the final table fair.

Live leaderboard

Ranked by points, then point difference, then wins — updating after every match.

Match timer

A round countdown that beeps and vibrates when time's up — and the screen stays awake while you score.

Works offline

No account, no internet. Auto-saves every 2 seconds and nothing leaves your phone.

Archives, export & backup

Every tournament saved with full scoreboards. Replay a line-up in one tap, export PDF or CSV, share the standings, or back it all up to a file.

Good to know

Questions people ask.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There's no sign-up and no account, and it works fully offline. Everything stays on your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Smash! is Android-only for now. An iPhone version may follow later, if it turns out to be possible.

What does it cost?

A one-time $2 on Google Play. No subscription, no ads, and nothing locked behind a paywall.

What if someone arrives late or gets injured?

Add a late player at any time and they play on until they've caught up. Take an injured player out of the draw, and bring them back later if they recover. When games end up uneven, Fair Ending projects the missing games from each player's average, so the final table stays fair.

How many courts and players can I run?

One court or ten. If you have more players than there are places on court, Smash! rotates who rests, so everyone plays about the same amount.

Can I play to 11 or 15, like a normal game?

Yes. Choose a race to a target score (win by 2, like a real game) or a fixed total of points per match, and a round timer if you want one.

Can I keep or share the results?

Yes. Every tournament is saved with its full scoreboard. Export a PDF or CSV, share the standings, replay a line-up in one tap, or back everything up to a file.

Does it have a dark theme?

Yes. Choose light, dark, or follow your phone's setting.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email smash@prekonsult.com. Every email is read.
